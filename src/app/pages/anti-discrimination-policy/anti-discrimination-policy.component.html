🛡️ Anti-Discrimination Policy

House Expert Solution Pvt. Ltd.

At House Expert Solution Pvt. Ltd., we are committed to providing a work and service environment that is free from any kind of discrimination. We strictly prohibit discrimination based on caste, religion, gender, age, disability, marital status, nationality, language, or any other personal characteristic.

We promote equal opportunity in hiring, promotions, client dealings, and day-to-day operations. Disrespectful behavior, unfair treatment, or biased decisions will not be tolerated.

Any employee or customer who feels discriminated against is encouraged to report the issue to our management. All complaints will be treated confidentially and addressed promptly.

We believe in fair work, equal respect, and professional service for all.

 

2. Objective

This policy aims to:

  • Ensure a discrimination-free workplace and service experience.
  • Promote equal opportunity and fair treatment for all employees and customers.
  • Encourage mutual respect among employees and clients.

3. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All employees (permanent, temporary, or contractual)
  • Job applicants
  • Customers, vendors, and other stakeholders interacting with our business

4. Unacceptable Conduct

Discriminatory behaviors include, but are not limited to:

  • Biased hiring or promotion decisions
  • Derogatory remarks or jokes based on personal traits
  • Unequal distribution of tasks or opportunities
  • Harassment, exclusion, or unfair treatment based on social background or appearance

5. Complaint Procedure

Anyone who feels discriminated against can raise their concern confidentially through:

  • Reporting to a supervisor or manager
  • Contacting the HR department
  • Emailing the grievance redressal team at support@houseexpert.in

Every complaint will be investigated with confidentiality, neutrality, and urgency.

6. Consequences for Policy Violation

Any employee or associate found guilty of discrimination will face:

  • Written warnings
  • Suspension
  • Termination of contract
  • Legal action if necessary

7. Protection Against Retaliation

House Expert Packers and Movers Pvt. Ltd. ensures zero tolerance toward retaliation. Any form of retaliation against individuals who report discrimination or assist in an investigation will be treated as a serious offense.

8. Commitment to Equality

We are proud to serve a diverse customer base across India. Every customer—regardless of their background—is entitled to equal treatment, quality service, and respectful communication from our team.

9. Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed annually and updated as necessary to reflect legal requirements and best practices.
